About Ibiza Mirissa

A tropical stage for beachside nightlife.

Set in Mirissa, Sri Lanka, Ibiza Mirissa blends the ease of a coastal escape with the pulse of a modern night venue. Days drift from poolside drinks into sunset soundtracks, then lift into DJ-led evenings, live music and private celebrations.

The experience is polished but relaxed: warm lights, signature drinks, tropical textures and a social atmosphere built for travelers, locals and groups looking for a night that feels memorable from the first arrival.
